The Shrike

Ongoing Series

A freelancer of no country and no file, hired through cutouts by agencies with positions they can't advertise.

He takes the jobs nobody can afford to own, and he is the best alive at becoming other people. Therein lies the problem: somewhere beneath the aliases, the man himself has gone missing.

Nobody knows his name. Nobody knows why he lights a candle in every city, or what the chalk bird means.

But he does.
